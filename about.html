About the Project

Traffic Safety & Collision Hotspots is our final project designed to help users better understand where, when, and how often transportation collisions occur across Seattle, Washington. The application visualizes incidents involving cyclists, pedestrians, and motorists using GeoJSON datasets, heatmaps, time filters, and interactive mapping tools.

Our dashboard allows users to examine spatial and temporal trends, identify high-risk areas, and explore collision frequencies. A severity key on the map (bottom-left) helps users interpret the seriousness of each collision plotted.

The primary audience includes urban planners, transportation analysts, public safety advocates, community members, and students or researchers interested in digital geographies and spatial equity. Traffic safety is a continuous issue, and our aim is to make collision patterns more visually compelling — encouraging viewers to understand the seriousness of these patterns beyond traditional statistics.
